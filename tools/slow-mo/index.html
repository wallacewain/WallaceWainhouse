Slow-Mo Studio

Phones record at 120 or 240 fps but often share back at normal speed — so your slow motion vanishes. This bakes the slow motion in. Pick an output frame rate, chop the timeline into chunks at real speed, half or full slow-mo, ramp between speeds, and smooth it all with motion interpolation. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

How it works: your captured frames are re-timed so they play slowly (that's the true, sharp slow-mo), and where a chunk is slower than your source can cover, motion interpolation synthesises the in-between frames. Audio is dropped — slow-mo audio isn't meaningful. Output is H.264 MP4. The first render downloads the processing engine (~32 MB) and then caches it. Everything is local; your video never leaves your device. Want to trim first, or pull the audio out? Try the Video Trimmer or MP3 Extractor.
