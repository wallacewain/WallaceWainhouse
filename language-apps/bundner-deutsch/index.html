Bündner Deutsch

Swiss German as spoken in Graubünden, real recordings
iPhone and AndroidFreeIn the browser, nothing to install

Swiss German as it's spoken in Graubünden (around Chur and St. Moritz), not standard German. You hear a phrase in English, say it out loud in Swiss German, then hear a real Bündner say it.

Open Bündner Deutsch

What's in it

  • 493 phrases recorded by native speakers from Graubünden (the SwissDial recordings, ETH Zürich), not a computer voice: there isn't one for the dialect.
  • Everyday phrases first, each with a word-for-word line so you can see how the dialect is built.
  • Spaced repetition: each phrase comes back at growing gaps, just before you'd forget it.

Get it

  1. Open Bündner Deutsch on your phone. It runs in the browser on iPhone and Android: nothing to install, no account.
  2. Add it to your home screen so it opens like an app. iPhone: in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". Android: in Chrome, tap ⋮, then "Add to Home screen".
  3. Save it for offline: in its Settings, tap "Save for offline" so it works with no signal.

How to use it

  • Say it out loud in the gap, then listen to the answer.
  • No voice commands: tap the screen if you missed it. It's taught again and comes back sooner. Otherwise it moves on by itself.
  • Headphone buttons work: ⏮ means missed it, ⏭ skips ahead. Keep the page open while it plays: iPhones pause web pages when they're locked.

Your progress

Your progress is kept on your phone, in the browser. Back it up now and then in Settings: deleting the home screen icon or clearing the browser's data loses it. Restore puts it back, on the same phone or a new one.

Before you start: give your first sessions at least 30 minutes of gentle focus.

The first few minutes can feel overwhelming. That's completely normal, so please don't give up then: stick with it for the full half hour and it starts to fall into place.

Don't worry at all if it feels like nothing is going in. That's normal, and it's fine. Just relax completely and trust the app to do the work: it brings every phrase back at the right moment, again and again, until it sticks. You don't need to try hard, take notes or remember anything on purpose. Just listen, have a go out loud, and keep going.
