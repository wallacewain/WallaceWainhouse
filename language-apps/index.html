Learn to speak, hands-free

Free apps for Slovak, Cantonese, Spanish, French and Swiss German, plus one for fluent French speakers who want to sound native. You hear a phrase in English, say it out loud in the language, then hear a native speaker say it. It works while you drive, walk or cook, with your phone in your pocket.

Each person who joins gets the apps closer to a proper public release.

How they work

  1. Hear a phrase in English.
  2. Say it out loud in the language. Have a go even if you're not sure: guessing is part of learning.
  3. Hear a native speaker say it. The app moves on by itself.

New phrases come one or two at a time, mixed in with ones you've already met, and each comes back at growing gaps, just before you'd forget it. The phrases are what people really say, in the order you'll actually hear them. No account, no ads, and your progress stays on your phone.

Each app's page has screenshots, what's in it, how to get it and how to use it.

Before you start: give your first sessions at least 30 minutes of gentle focus.

The first few minutes can feel overwhelming. That's completely normal, so please don't give up then: stick with it for the full half hour and it starts to fall into place.

Don't worry at all if it feels like nothing is going in. That's normal, and it's fine. Just relax completely and trust the app to do the work: it brings every phrase back at the right moment, again and again, until it sticks. You don't need to try hard, take notes or remember anything on purpose. Just listen, have a go out loud, and keep going.
