Learn to speak, hands-free

Free apps for Slovak, Cantonese, Spanish, French and Swiss German. You hear a phrase in English, say it out loud in the language, then hear a native speaker say it. It works while you drive, walk or cook, with your phone in your pocket.

Before you start: give your first sessions at least 30 minutes of gentle focus.

The first few minutes can feel overwhelming. That's completely normal, so please don't give up then: stick with it for the full half hour and it starts to fall into place.

Don't worry at all if it feels like nothing is going in. That's normal, and it's fine. Just relax completely and trust the app to do the work: it brings every phrase back at the right moment, again and again, until it sticks. You don't need to try hard, take notes or remember anything on purpose. Just listen, have a go out loud, and keep going.

Bündner Deutsch

Swiss German as spoken in Graubünden, real recordings
  1. Open Bündner Deutsch on your phone. It works on iPhone and Android, nothing to install.
  2. Add it to your home screen (iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen).
  3. In Settings, tap Save for offline so it works with no signal.
No voice commands: it gives you time to answer, then plays the answer. Tap the screen if you missed it. Keep the page open while it plays, and back up your progress in Settings now and then.

If the test page says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and open it again. After installing, open the app once and allow the microphone and notifications.

Then practise the three command words in Settings before your first session: tap a command to hear how it's said, then say it yourself and the app shows whether it understood you.

How it works

  1. Press play. You hear a phrase in English.
  2. Say it out loud in the language. Have a go even if you're not sure: guessing is part of learning.
  3. Hear a native speaker say it. Repeat it after them if you like. The app listens for your answer and moves on by itself.

New phrases come one or two at a time, mixed in with ones you've already met. Each phrase comes back at growing gaps, just before you'd forget it. That's why you don't need to try to remember: the app does the remembering for you.

Later on you'll hear short conversations and longer passages at natural speed. They're built up slowly and in chunks first, and they only play once you know every word in them.

Talking to the app

  • Didn't know it, or got it wrong? Say the "don't know" word for your app (see your app above). It'll teach it again and bring it back soon. There's no penalty: missing things is how the app knows what to practise.
  • Too easy? Say the "easy" word and it won't come back so often.
  • Want to hear it again? Say the "again" word.
  • Practise the commands in Settings: tap one to hear it, then say it and check the app understands you.

In the car, or with headphones

  • It works over Bluetooth and with your steering-wheel or headphone buttons: previous means you missed it, next skips ahead.
  • Keep your eyes on the road: everything works by voice and buttons, so you never need to look at the screen.

Settings worth knowing

  • Pace: how many new phrases a day. If it feels like too much, turn it down. You'll learn more from fewer phrases that stick.
  • Level: where new material starts, if you already know some of the language.
  • Bedtime: a short last round of the day's trickiest phrases, then it stops.

Private and offline

No account, no ads and no internet needed. Everything runs on your phone, including the speech recognition. Your progress stays on your phone and is kept through updates.
